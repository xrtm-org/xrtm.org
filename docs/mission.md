# The Strategy

While our **Manifesto** outlines our vision ("Forecasting is Intelligence"), the **Strategy** defines how we get there.

## 1. Vertical Integration
We are building the entire stack—Data, Inference, Evaluation, and Training. By controlling the vertical, we ensure **Zero Leakage** at every step. We don't rely on third-party tools that might accidentally fetch live web data during a backtest.

## 2. Open Source First
We believe that the future of intelligence belongs to everyone. By making our core infrastructure Open Source, we invite the global community to verify our rigorous standards and contribute to the "Grand Challenge" of predicting the future.

## 3. Institutional Grade
We are not building toys. Our focus is on:
*   **Reliability:** Strict typing and high test coverage.
*   **Auditability:** Every prediction leaves a trace.
*   **Sovereignty:** You own your data and your models.

## 4. The Loop
Our ultimate strategy is to close the loop between Prediction and Reality. Each failed forecast is a training example. Each success is a benchmark. By iterating this loop faster than anyone else, xrtm evolves towards higher intelligence.
